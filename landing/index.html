One install · every harness · every prompt

Your AI has hundreds of tools. Toutur makes it choose like it knows them.

Toutur reads every prompt the moment it arrives — on your machine, in ~250 ms — and hands your agent a ranked shortlist of the skills, MCPs and subagents that actually fit. It learns from what gets used, heals its own wiring after harness updates, and stays silent when nothing fits.

Plain English

A concierge between you and your agents

You describe the job in your own words. Toutur quietly slips the assistant a note — exactly which of its tools fit this job, ranked, with reasons. Nothing fits? It says nothing.

Retrieval

Two lanes, one answer

Every prompt is scored by a lexical lane (BM25, generic-word damping, stack awareness) and a semantic lane (local embedding vectors), then fused — so exact names and fuzzy intent both win.

Learning

Learns from use

Every suggestion actually loaded feeds back. Hits earn slowly, misses decay fast — one lucky match can never entrench.

Authority

Self-healing wiring

Harness update wipes the hooks? The next route detects it, rewrites the mandate, re-lays the shim, logs the repair — on every call.

Enforcement

75% adoption floor

Suggestions are advice — until they're ignored. Below the floor the gate denies edits until routed skills load. Above it, it stays quiet.

Local-first

No cloud, no keys, no telemetry

Stdlib Python plus one local embedding model. Nothing leaves the machine; the whole brain runs between your keystroke and the model's first token.

One-liner

Installs everywhere it finds

One command probes every harness on the box, wires each with the right interceptor, writes the mandate into its rulebook, and shows exactly what changed.

Under the hood

Every call, end to end

Intercept → verify authority → retrieve on two lanes → shape the card → deliver → learn. Every stage degrades to the previous stage's output; nothing on this path can block your message.
