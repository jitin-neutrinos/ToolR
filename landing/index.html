The anatomy, drawn

What runs on every call, how the modules relate, and why two retrieval lanes beat one — all three maps carry the measured numbers, not the marketing ones.

Every call, end to end

Intercept, verify authority, retrieve on two lanes, shape the card, deliver, learn. Every stage degrades to the previous stage's output; nothing on this path can block your message.

The pipeline

what happens on every single call
1

Intercept

A per-prompt hook or plugin hands the raw prompt to route.py. A 90-second cache serves repeat prompts without rescoring.

2

Verify authority

The mandate and interceptor are checked on every call. Anything a harness update undid is rewritten and logged before routing continues.

3

Retrieve on two lanes

BM25 with generic-word damping scores exact names; local embeddings score fuzzy intent. The blend is convex, fused at α = 0.5, with an exact-name guarantee.

4

Shape the card

Score floor, tail cut and kind quota pick the shortlist; a score-cliff and diversity pass keep the card short and honest.

5

Deliver

The card rides into the prompt before the model sees it. Every stage fails open, so a dead lane can never delay a message.

6

Learn and enforce

Loads feed the prior, off the hot path. Below the 75% adoption floor the gate denies edits until routed skills load; above it, it stays quiet.
